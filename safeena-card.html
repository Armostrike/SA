For Safeena

Our little dream

✦ ✦ ✦

My love,

I’m only 21, and sometimes when I think about our future, I realize how much of our life is still waiting for us.

We are still young. We are still learning, growing, studying, working, making mistakes, and trying to understand what life has planned for us. We don't have everything today, and honestly, I don't expect us to.

But I have a dream.

A dream that I want to build slowly, together with you.

One day, I want to travel the world with you. 🌍❤️

Not today. Maybe not tomorrow. Maybe it will take years.

First, we have to work hard.

I have to build my career as a Diagnostics Engineer. I have to keep learning, improve myself, take more responsibilities and grow financially. Today I may be earning around ₹90,000, and hopefully, with experience and hard work, that number will grow in the coming years.

And you have your own journey too. You are studying, building your knowledge and preparing yourself for your future.

I don't want either of us to give up our dreams.

I want us to become successful together.

Maybe there will be a time when we have to choose between buying something we want and saving for something we need. Maybe there will be months when we can't travel because we have responsibilities. Maybe I'll come home tired from work, and you'll be exhausted from your studies.

But even on those days, I want us to remember our little dream.

“Ek din hum duniya ghoomne jayenge.”

Maybe we'll start small. One trip. Then another. Then maybe one day we'll look at our passports and realize we've visited places we once only saw on Instagram and YouTube.

  • I want to see the mountains with you.
  • I want to see snow for the first time with you.
  • I want to sit beside you on a beach and watch the sunset.
  • I want to walk with you through streets where neither of us knows the language.

I want us to try different foods, take thousands of pictures, make stupid videos, get lost somewhere, argue about directions, and then laugh about it together.

I want to experience those moments with you, not alone.

Because honestly, the place doesn't matter that much to me.

You matter.
🗼 Paris would be beautiful, but it would mean more if you're holding my hand.
🏔️ Switzerland would be amazing, but I'd still be looking at your smile.
🏝️ The Maldives would have beautiful sunsets, but I'd want to sit beside you and watch them together.

Because I don't simply want to see the world.

I want to experience life with you.

And I know we have a lot of work to do before that dream becomes reality. So let's take our time.

You study.
I'll work.
We both grow.
We save money.
We support our families.
We build our careers.
We become financially stronger.

And whenever life gets difficult, let's remind each other that we're building something bigger than today's problems.

Main chahta hoon ki hum dono apne sapne poore karein.

I don't want to promise you that life will always be easy. I can't promise that we'll always have money. I can't promise that there won't be problems.

But I can promise that I'll keep working toward the life we dream about.

And I want you beside me while I do it.

Maybe someday, when we're older, we'll sit together and look at our old photographs. We'll see the two young people we were at 21. We'll remember how we didn't have everything. We'll remember how we worked hard. We'll remember how we waited.

And we'll smile because we finally turned our little dream into reality.

Then I'll hold your hand and say,

“Dekho jaan… humne sach mein kar dikhaya.” ❤️

Until that day, let's keep growing. Let's keep believing. Let's keep supporting each other. And let's never stop dreaming.

Because we have our whole life ahead of us.

✦ ✦ ✦

Mai tumse bahut pyaar karta hoon.

And one day, when we're ready...
you, me, our bags, our passports, and the whole world waiting for us. 🌍✈️❤️

Abhi mehnat karte hain jaan… duniya baad mein ghoomenge. ❤️
Forever yours,
Ameen
